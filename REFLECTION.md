# Typography Enhancement Reflection
**Project**: Poetry Interpretation - "Phenomenal Woman" by Maya Angelou  
**Student**: Olivia D.  
**Course**: Web Development 2

## How Font Choices Enhance the Poem

My typography choices for Maya Angelou's "Phenomenal Woman" were carefully selected to amplify the poem's theme of strong, elegant, confident beautiful women.

I chose **Crimson Pro** as the primary font because of its readability. It helps me express certain parts of the poem that I feel should be emphasized, which gives the poem a more direct vision of how her words are impactful and hold boundless meaning. I used light weights (300-400) for introspective moments ("Pretty women wonder where my secret lies"), medium weights (500-600) for building anticipation ("I say,"), and bold weights (700-800) for the triumphant refrain ("Phenomenal woman, That's me"). It gives the poem a quiet yet bold transition from each word and the feeling it gives when reading.

## Lighthouse Accessibility Audit Learnings

Through the accessibility audit, I discovered my initial gold color (#B8860B) fell short of WCAG AA standards. This taught me accessibility isn't just about good intentions—it requires measurable standards and testing. I implemented `font-display: swap` and preloaded critical fonts to prevent layout shift. Typography performance directly impacts user experience.

## Performance Trade-offs Discovered

Variable fonts provide better performance despite initial complexity, resulting in faster loading with more design flexibility. I preloaded only essential fonts for above-the-fold content, balancing immediate display needs with resource optimization.

This project gave me a new perspective on how typography can be used in such a powerful way. A great tool for enhancing meaning, not just making text pretty or stand out but give substance and bringing her words to life. Maya Angelou's words deserve to be highlighted and cherished seeing how this particular poem can withstand all evolution of women no matter past or present with continued relevance.
